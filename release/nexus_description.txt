[size=5][b]Ultimate Inventory Overhaul[/b][/size]

If you're a collector like me, you probably get sick of scrolling through 3,000 items trying to figure out what to sell. So I rebuilt all the inventory screens.

This isn't a sort-order mod. I went into the actual Flash files for the inventory, the trade screen, the loot/stash screen and the HUD and changed how they work. As far as I know nobody has done the inventory this way before. Nothing touches your save and nothing changes what Henry can carry or do. It just shows you stuff the game already knows.

[size=4][b]Headers[/b][/size]
Every tab gets split under headers that actually mean something. Armor is grouped by the slot it goes in, and the number tells you which pieces compete for the same slot. They are also ordered by the order you equip them in so you're not constantly scrolling back up or down to equip a gambeson or coif or padded leg hoses:

[list]
[*]Head I Coifs / Head II Helmets, Hats / Head III Collars / Head IV Hoods
[*]Body I Shirts, Gambesons / Body II Mail / Body III Plate / Body IV Outerwear
[*]Arms I Sleeves / Arms II Gloves / Waist I Belts / Waist II Pouches
[*]Legs I Hose / Legs II Leg armour / Feet I Boots / Feet II Spurs
[*]Neck I Necklaces, Brooches, Spectacles / Fingers I Rings
[*]Horse: Saddles, Bridles, Chanfrons, Caparisons, Breeching, Horseshoes
[/list]

The weapons are split by type: Longswords, Short swords, Hunting swords, Sabres, Axes, Maces, Polearms, Daggers, Shields, Bows, Arrows, Crossbows, Bolts, Handgonnes, Shot, and Torches. Food gets split by Dried, Smoked, Cooked, Baked goods, Dairy and eggs, Fruit, Vegetables, Raw meat, Raw fish, Spices and honey, Beer, Wine, Liquor, Water, Saviour Schnapps, potions by strength, Special potions, Poisons. Books, materials and the Other tab get the same treatment (Skill books, Recipes, Blacksmith plans, Herbs, Dried herbs, Repair kits, Dice, Keys, and so on). About 90 headers total. Collapse them with L3 like vanilla.

If you're new you stop guessing what goes where. If you're not, you stop scrolling endlessly.

[size=4][b]Color variants get one row[/b][/size]
You've got six colors of the same gambeson? Now it's one row with a + in front. That row shows:

[list]
[*]A number in parentheses after the name. That's how many different variants of the item you own.
[*]The count column is the total pieces across all of them in a collapsed row. So if your total pieces is greater than the different variants you own, that means you have extras.
[*]Weight and price are totals for everything inside until you expand the row.
[*]Condition is the average, but the color comes from the WORST piece inside. Red row = something in there is wrecked.
[*]Stat and grade are the best piece inside.
[*]A * after the name means a unique item variant is hiding in there.
[/list]

If you open up a collapsed row, every piece is listed with a - in front so you know you're inside a group. Close it and it folds back up. You can also expand or collapse everything at once if you want the plain old flat list back. This is if you want to sort every single piece by condition or strength. Food, potions, herbs, anything that comes in different conditions gets grouped the same way. Weapons group by name, so all your Longswords sit in one row no matter what condition they're in. If two different items share a name they get V1 and V2 so they don't get mixed up.

[size=4][b]HAVE (#)[/b][/size]
When you're at a trader, looting a body, or opening a chest (not the stash), every item you own shows a HAVE (n) in the column next to the amount they have. That's how many you already have between Henry, the horse and your stash, with the best grade you own drawn next to it. If you see a Steel skullcap (III) on the ground, the row says HAVE (1) with a (I) next to it, you know it's an upgrade without riding home to check.

At traders I put HAVE in the equipped column, which is empty there anyway, and moved the divider so it reads as its own column. You still have to expand the trader's rows if they have multiple variants of the same item to see what you already have.

[size=4][b]Reading the rows[/b][/size]
Some things that aren't obvious until you've used it a bit:

[list]
[*]If the number in parentheses is lower than the count column, you've got spares. (3) and a count of 7 means seven pieces in three colors. Four of those are duplicates. Sell them.
[*]If the number in parentheses matches the count, you've got one of each.
[*]Group row condition is orange or red but the average looks fine? One bad piece is dragging it down. Open it, it's the lowest one.
[*]Star on the group row? Open it before you sell the lot. There's a unique in there.
[*]Is the trader's piece a higher grade than your HAVE mark? Upgrade.
[*]Highlighting a group row previews the top piece on Henry and shows its stats like a normal row would.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Controls[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Keyboard[/b]: E or Enter on a group row opens or closes it. V closes the group you're in from any row inside it. G opens or closes everything. On the stash, loot and horse screens, hold E on a group row to move the whole group over at once.
[*][b]Controller[/b]: A on a group row opens it. B inside an open group closes it instead of kicking you out of the menu. B on a normal row still exits like always. Hold Y to expand all or collapse all. On the stash, loot and horse screens, hold A on a group row to move the whole group over at once.
[*]The button hints at the bottom change depending on what row you're on, so they say Expand, Collapse, Expand all, or Collapse all when that's what the button does.
[*]Group rows show the basket mark at traders like any other row.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Names[/b][/size]
I kept the original names. No renaming for taste, no lore touched. What I did fix is the stuff that was just wrong: "Gambeson long" and "Gambeson short" are now Long gambeson and Short gambeson like every other long and short thing in the game, Courtier's boots got its apostrophe back, Courtier's pourpoint lost the stray period on the end, and the executioner's caparison was spelled two different ways with two different apostrophes so the two could never sort together. 19 entries total. English only, other languages are untouched.

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
Main file: install with Vortex, or drop the zz_inventory_overhaul folder into the game's Mods folder.

Controller plugin (optional file): extract into the game folder so you get Bin\Win64MasterMasterSteamPGO\Inventory_Overhaul.asi. It needs [b]Ultimate ASI Loader[/b] to get loaded. Keyboard and mouse work without it.

If you have A Real Sorted Inventory or Sorted Inventory, remove them, this does both their jobs but better I believe. To uninstall, delete the mod folder and the .asi. Your save is untouched either way.

I tested this on Steam with an Xbox Elite Series 2 controller and keyboard. The plugin hooks both input paths the game uses (GameInput and XInput). PlayStation pads that go through Steam Input look like XInput to the game so they should be fine. If you run a DualSense without Steam Input, let me know how it goes, I don't have one to test.

[size=4][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]
This replaces the inventory, transfer and selection screens, the HUD and the English item name table. The game takes whole files, last mod loaded wins, so:

[list]
[*]It won't work with other inventory UI mods (sorting, filters, trader tweaks), HUD mods that ship their own hud.gfx, and mods that rename items or replace the English localization.
[*]These should be fine: stat and balance mods, new items, textures, models, camera mods, the camping mod and its compatibility patch. New items just land under whatever header their slot and icon put them in.
[/list]

If you want to make a compatibility patch for something, the source is linked below.

[size=4][b]Known issues[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]A chest within about four meters of one of your stash chests gets treated as the stash, so no HAVE on it.
[*]The stash screen micro stutters every few seconds (around 15 for me) if you're a hoarder. That's the vanilla game because it does it on a clean install too.
[/list]

Found a bug? Attach kcd.log and Bin\Win64MasterMasterSteamPGO\Inventory_Overhaul.log.
